1-1 PComp Art: Rock Band
Rock Band was made by Harmonix Music Systems, who previously created the Guitar Hero games. Due to the previous development of Guitar Hero they were familiar with the guitar and bass controls. The main development was for the drum system they created to complete the Rock Band experience. Rock Band is a family and friends rhythm game, designed to be easy enough for children on the lower end and be hard enough for core rhythm game players on the hard end. The core games allow for 4 instruments being, Guitar, Bass, Drums, and Vocals. Although in the 3rd installation they added pro instruments and a keytar.

The experience has a wide range of senses and feelings as being able to play a song perfect on expert mode is very rewarding. On the other hand the feeling of playing drums can quickly become painful if you don't have the proper grip on the drumsticks in addition to the kick drum pedal being quite stiff leading to pain and fatigue quickly. This in addition to improper syncing can lead to frustration but it only makes the payoff even greater when you do good or are able to move up in difficulty. But overall being able to play a music game with family or friends where you can feel like you are actually preforming a song like a proper band is quite incredible.
